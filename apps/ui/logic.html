Grounded in the five-year strategy to introduce AI and transform internal architecture and product lines. AI is positioned for efficiency and business growth, not headcount reduction.

Core argument

Align on the challenge, approach, and advantage before the audience-specific walkthrough.

Dimension Core thesis
Business challenge Departmental AI silos: redundant builds, disconnected data, and high-risk actions without a shared control gate.
Solution approach Platform-standard operating loops and tool boundaries; departments deliver capability packs only; cross-department read/write via shared information.
Comparative advantage More governable than a single universal entry; less duplication than departmental stacks; more auditable than agent-to-agent handoff; business-testable and IT-observable.

Audience validation targets

Logic pages provide the outline; business and IT views prove usage value and governance value.

Business Lead Validation target: multi-type capabilities are runnable; cross-department risk control via shared information (ticket fill → outreach block).
IT Ops Validation target: one platform foundation is observable and diagnosable; tools and data access are governable.

01 · Strategy and org analysis

Clarify permissions, shared-write/outreach red lines, audit duty, in/out of scope for this phase. See Part 3.
