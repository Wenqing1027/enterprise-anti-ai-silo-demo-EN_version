# Agent QA SOP Highlights (Synthetic)

## Required checks
1. Verify VIN and model;
2. Restate user issue and confirm;
3. State next step and timeline;
4. Avoid absolute promises ("definitely", "absolutely no problem").

## Risk phrase examples
- "Absolutely no problem", "guaranteed fixed in three days", "official full refund for sure"

Hitting risk phrases → QA fail, enter coaching list.
