# Store VI and Non-Exclusive Red Lines (Synthetic)

## Prohibited
- Mixed unauthorized competitor vehicles in same retail area (`TAG-non-exclusive-display`);
- Unauthorized changes to storefront primary color and standard fonts (`TAG-vi-violation`);
- Expired posters or wrong price tag templates.

## Inspection actions
1. Vision/inspection produces flagged report;
2. Create remediation ticket with `due_date` and `verify_method`;
3. Two consecutive P0 violations may affect diamond-tier rebate eligibility.
