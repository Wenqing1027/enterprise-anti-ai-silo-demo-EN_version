# Brake Noise and Pad Replacement Guide (Synthetic)

## Symptoms
- Light-brake metallic friction; heavy brake normal.
- Squeal on first brake after rain, then disappears.

## Assessment
1. Ask mileage and recent maintenance;
2. Visual pad thickness check (threshold: replace if <2 mm);
3. Rule out embedded grit and slight disc rust after rain (common, not a fault).

## Ticket field requirements
- `fault_category=brake`
- `desc_text` must include: noise scenario (light/heavy brake/after rain), mileage, subjective braking distance impact.
