# Charging Port and Charger Compatibility (Synthetic)

## Principles
- Only Qingshu-certified charger models allowed (example: QS-CHG-48-xx).
- Non-OEM fast chargers may cause slow charging, temperature alerts, or void warranty.

## Troubleshooting
1. Swap same-spec charger for trial;
2. Inspect charging port pins for burn marks or looseness;
3. Check telemetry for `CHG_OT_01`.
